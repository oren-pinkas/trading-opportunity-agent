## Researched 2026-07-25T06:15:22Z

Three-round panel (bull/bear/quant, synthesized) converged unanimously on **no-trade**.
The dossier's 2026-09-30 impact_window was rejected as a comment-period artifact with
no price-forming mechanism (likely mis-derived — a 60-day window from the 2026-07-02
release closes ~2026-09-21, not 09-30); the real catalyst is the CY2027 OPPS final
rule, historically landing ~2026-10-30 to 11-02, not yet published. Verified pricing
(`toa price --provider twelvedata`) showed SGRY rose on the rule's release day and only
began a ~9% decline six days later — temporally disconnected from the rule and not
statistically distinguishable from noise (t -0.86 to -0.59) given SGRY's realized
41-60% annualized vol. UHS (the more direct OPPS payee) moved far less than SGRY (a
mostly ASC-fee-schedule name with minimal direct OPPS exposure) — a cross-section
inconsistent with any single coherent rule-driven mechanism. Full transcript:
[transcript.md](./transcript.md).
