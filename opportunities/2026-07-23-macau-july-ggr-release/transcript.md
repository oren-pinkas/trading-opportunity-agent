## Institutional lessons injected (economic type; not Macau-specific)

1. Anchor entry to a live pre-event quote, not the research-day price; if the live price has drifted >~0.5-1% from the plan anchor, re-derive targets/EV or void the trade rather than filling blind. (2026-07-06, ISM mfg)
2. When the thesis is "catalyst reprices X higher" and X has already rallied to its 52-week high before the event, treat the move as priced-in: fade or shrink, don't chase the entry. (2026-07-06, ISM mfg)
