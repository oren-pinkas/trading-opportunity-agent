## Researched 2026-07-25T10:40:05Z — NO-TRADE

Three-round panel (bull/bear sonnet, quant opus; synthesizer opus). The panel found no
tradable edge in NUE from EU CBAM/ETS Phase 5 reform. CBAM taxes EU importers, not a
US-domiciled EAF producer -- Nucor's EU export exposure is a rounding error against its
revenue base, and the theoretical certificate cost on a hypothetical EU-bound ton
(EUR 75.28/tCO2 on a ~USD 900/t product) is under 5 percent, applied to a de-minimis
volume share. The July 17 proposal was 8 days stale at research time and the Council
deal is not enacted law -- no binding vote or implementing act falls inside the window.
The BULL opened long (confidence 55) on a "carbon-advantaged EAF beneficiary" narrative
but conceded in Round 2 that the direct-transmission critique lands, cutting confidence
to 25 and admitting no evidence yet that the narrative is actually forming (no cited
sell-side note, no fund flow data). The BEAR held firm at no-trade throughout, calling
the dossier's framing a likely mislabeled catalyst. The QUANT was decisive: base rate
10-15% for an attributable move, signal-to-noise ~0.11-0.15 (at or below the 0.15
non-durable threshold from a prior post-mortem), net EV ~-0.08% after costs, and no
defensible precise entry/exit timestamp exists since 2026-09-30 is an arbitrary
calendar boundary rather than an event resolution. Verdict: NO-TRADE, entry/exit left
null rather than inventing a timestamp. Full debate with citations in `transcript.md`.
